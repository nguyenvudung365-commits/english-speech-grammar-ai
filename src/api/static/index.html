Local processing & deletion policy (local-v1)

Audio and text are processed only on this computer; no external inference services or training. Email is used for login. Private WAV files are kept under the configured local storage directory, not publicly served. Maximum audio: 30 seconds and 10 MiB.

Run the separate cleanup scheduler: it removes audio and all session content after 24 hours (text-only sessions expire after 24 hours too). While the computer or scheduler is off, scheduled deletion cannot run; overdue content is removed on its next run. Delete a session or account sooner whenever needed.

No anonymization has been validated, so this demo deletes expired content even if optional retention is selected. No backups are configured by the demo; any operator-created backups may retain deleted data until they expire. Do not upload sensitive or unconsented speech.
